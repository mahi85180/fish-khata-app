Fish Khata — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

What data the app handles

Fish Khata stores your business records (parties, bills, payments, expenses, stock and settings) on your own device. We do not run any server and we do not collect, sell or share your data.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you turn on Google Sync, the app asks for permission to the Google Drive app data folder only (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata). The app uses it only to store and read one file with your own Fish Khata data, so that your devices signed in with the same Google account show the same records. The app cannot see any other files in your Google Drive. You can turn sync off at any time from the app, and you can remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Fish Khata's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Activation

To activate, the app shows a Device Code that you send to us on WhatsApp. We use it only to generate your activation key.

Update check

The app reads a small public file from GitHub to know if a new version is available. No personal data is sent.
